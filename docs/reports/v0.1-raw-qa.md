## Invariants

Zero by construction: `validate_bars(..., raise_on_error=True)` runs on every series before it is written, so a file that broke an invariant would not be in the tier to be counted. This row exists so its absence is deliberate rather than forgotten.

Off-grid bars are counted above rather than rejected. They are real bars published on a shifted phase after an exchange restart — 43 consecutive hourly bars on spot BTCUSDT from 2018-02-09, each still exactly one hour after the last. Snapping them to the grid would be imputation, which the raw tier does not do (ADR-0010).

## Eyeball pass

Numbers a report can produce and still be wrong about. These were checked by hand against what
is known about the exchange.

| Series | Rows | Range | Span | Gaps | Off-grid |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| spot 1d BTCUSDT | 3,290 | 2017-08-17 → 2026-08-19 | 3,289 d | 0 | 0 |
| spot 1h BTCUSDT | 78,829 | 2017-08-17 → 2026-08-19 | 3,289.8 d | 127 | 43 |
| spot 1d ETHUSDT | 3,290 | 2017-08-17 → 2026-08-19 | 3,289 d | 0 | 0 |
| um 1d BTCUSDT | 2,423 | 2020-01-01 → 2026-08-19 | 2,422 d | 0 | 0 |
| um 1h BTCUSDT | 58,152 | 2020-01-01 → 2026-08-19 | 2,423 d | 0 | 0 |
| um 1d SOLUSDT | 2,161 | 2020-09-14 → 2026-08-19 | 2,165 d | 5 | 0 |

**Listing ages match.** BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT spot both start 2017-08-17, which is when Binance
opened. `3290 = 3289 + 1` is exactly one daily bar per day inclusive, so nothing is missing and
nothing is doubled. USDT-M perpetuals start 2020-01-01 — the exchange launched them in September
2019, and the bucket's monthly archives for `um` begin that January. SOLUSDT's perpetual starts
2020-09-14, which is when it was listed.

**Grid continuity for the majors.** `um` BTCUSDT is unbroken at both frequencies: 58,152 hourly
bars over 2,423 days is 2,423 × 24 with nothing missing. Spot BTCUSDT carries 127 hourly gaps
across nine years — the early-exchange outages of 2017 and 2018 — and none of them at daily
resolution, which is right: a day that lost four hours still produced a daily bar.

**The gappiest series are gappy for reasons.** `STRAXUSDT` (216 hourly gaps) is a low-liquidity
spot pair. `TLMUSDT` and `ICPUSDT` lead the perpetual list at 708 and 626, both contracts that
have been delisted and relisted. None of these are corpus defects; they are what v0.3's cleaning
pass is for, and the counts are recorded here so that pass has a baseline to be measured against.

**Storage.** 1,205 files totalling 0.57 GiB — 600 Parquet, 600 sidecars, three pull manifests and
a README. Well under the 2 GB budget and far under the 100 GB private tier.

## Cross-check against an independent implementation

ADR-0012's safety net. A custom fetcher's real risk is a systematic misunderstanding that every
test written by the same author would share, so the check is a second implementation:
`binance_historical_data`, by an unrelated author, over January to March 2024.

| Series | Our rows in window | Theirs | Bars compared on 2024-02-14 | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| spot 1h BTCUSDT | 2,184 | 2,184 | 24 | **AGREE** |
| spot 1h ETHUSDT | 2,184 | 2,184 | 24 | **AGREE** |
| spot 1h SOLUSDT | 2,184 | 2,184 | 24 | **AGREE** |

3/3. Row counts identical over the overlap — no bar invented, none dropped — and every compared
value exact on `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume` and `amount`.

Volumes were compared deliberately. A base-versus-quote mix-up is exactly the systematic error
this exists to catch, and it would have left every price in the table identical.
