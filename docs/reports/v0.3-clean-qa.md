### Expected patterns, checked

The plan named four things that should be true if cleaning is behaving. All four hold.

- **Crypto majors lose approximately nothing.** BTCUSDT loses 0.80 % and ETHUSDT 0.95 % at 1h,
  both entirely to `min_length` trimming short tails around outage boundaries. No jump cuts, no
  gap cuts.
- **Losses concentrate in the thin tail.** Every one of the twenty most-cut series is a US
  small-cap, preferred share or community bank. The equities slice loses 9.79 % overall while
  AAPL loses 0.11 %.
- **FX weekend gaps contribute zero to drop counts.** The gap rule dropped **0 bars** in every
  slice, which is true by construction — it partitions and never deletes. The meaningful version
  is fragmentation, and Dukascopy carries **89 gap splits per series** over roughly 1,200 weeks of
  history. A weekend being cut would put that near 1,200.
- **Stagnant and illiquid excisions cluster where trading is thin.** 16,587 stagnant runs excised
  across 12,425 equity series; 6,301 across 300 crypto series at 1h, concentrated in dead pairs.

**Every row above needs an investigation written against it before the v0.3 gate.**

### `raw/dukascopy/fx/1h/USDJPY.parquet` — 1.69 % — **data problem, correctly cut**

`axiom clean probe` found 72 unexpected gaps in this instrument, and one of them is **4,572 slots
wide** — a 190-day hole in the middle of the series. The gap rule cuts there, correctly, and the
short piece stranded on one side is dropped by `min_length`. The remaining 48 one-slot gaps are
scattered evenly across the trading day, which is ordinary feed dropout rather than a session the
rule has misunderstood.

Residual loss after the padding removal is 2,321 bars of 142,015 that ever traded. Accepted.

### `raw/dukascopy/commodity/1h/XAUUSD.parquet` — 5.61 % — **rule artifact, accepted with a ceiling**

Gold's declared settlement break covers 21:00–01:00 UTC, which absorbs 2,561 of its 3,774 missing
slots. The remainder sit in hours 17–20 (144 + 121 + 93 + 63), which is the pre-settlement thin
window rather than a declared break. Each one partitions the series and `min_length` then drops
the 8,005 bars stranded in fragments shorter than 256.

Widening the break to cover 17:00–01:00 would recover most of that, and would also stop the rule
cutting on a genuine four-hour outage in the most-watched hours of the metals day. The trade is
not obviously worth it for 5.6 % of one instrument, so it is **not** taken here and is recorded
instead: if the commodity tier ever matters more than it does at 0.8 % of the corpus, this is the
knob.

### `raw/dukascopy/commodity/1d/XAUUSD.parquet` — 6.25 % — **real market pathology**

At 1d the `24x5-cfd` session degrades to "weekdays are expected", so every commodity holiday the
vendor does not publish is an unexpected gap. Gold has more of them than FX does. 469 bars are
lost to `min_length` out of 7,500 across 26 years. Ordinary, and cheaper to accept than to
maintain a per-instrument commodity holiday calendar for a six-instrument tier.

All twenty: **real market pathology, correctly excised.** These instruments trade a handful of

fragment the series, and no fragment reaches the 128 bars a daily context window needs. `GTN-A` is
the one variation — `min_length` alone, no stagnant — meaning it fragmented on missing sessions
rather than frozen prices, which is the same thinness through a different rule.

None of them is a data problem, and none is a rule artifact. A model cannot learn a price process
from an instrument that does not have one.

## Total-return coverage

Under the measured verdict (`split_and_dividend_adjusted`, ADR-0019) `tr_close` is an identity for
every source in the corpus, so `axiom derive tr` writes `derived/tr_close/manifest.json` and no bar
files. Coverage is **100 % of the 12,425 Stooq series**, `tr_available = true` throughout, because
the identity branch needs no dividend events to be truthful.

**491** of those tickers additionally have captured corporate actions from yfinance. That number
matters only if a future re-audit flips the verdict to `split_adjusted`, at which point it becomes
the coverage ceiling and the other 11,934 become `tr_available = false`. Recorded now so the
number is not discovered later.
